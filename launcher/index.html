Questions

Good to know.

Does it send anything to the internet?

No. It only searches your own PC. The only time it goes online is when you choose a web search, and then it simply opens your browser.

Can I change Alt+Space?

Yes. Right-click the tray icon and pick another shortcut.

Will it slow my PC down?

No. It sits quietly in the tray and only wakes up when you press the shortcut.

How do I close it?

Right-click the Pitzki Launcher icon next to the clock and choose Quit.
