Which Pitzki is for you?

Three quick questions.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I use both at the same time?

Yes. They are two separate apps that run happily side by side. Install one, or both.

Do they cost anything?

No. Both are free, with no ads and no account.

Windows says it "protected your PC". Is that okay?

Windows says that for any app that doesn't come from a store. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

How do I close them?

Each one has a little icon next to the clock. Right-click it and choose Quit.

Where is my data?

Only on your computer. A few features ask free services for things like weather or lyrics, and each one can be switched off.

I have an idea or found a bug

Open an issue on Pitzki or Pitzki Widgets.
